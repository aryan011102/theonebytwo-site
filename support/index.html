Support

Write to us at pritika@theonebytwo.com. We answer within 24 hours. In the app, you can also go to You, Settings, Message the founder and write to us there.

Someone is bothering you

  • On their profile, tap ··· and choose Report profile. In a chat, tap ··· and choose Report. Reporting someone also blocks them: they can no longer see you or write to you.
  • You can block someone without reporting them from the same menu. Blocked people are listed in Settings, Blocked users.
  • Our moderators act on reports within 24 hours. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users: content that breaks our terms is removed, and so are the accounts behind it.
  • If you are in danger, call 112.

Your account

  • Signing in: theonebytwo signs you in with a code sent to your phone number. If the code does not arrive, wait a minute and ask for a new one.
  • Taking a break: Settings, Delete account offers Take a break instead, which hides your profile without deleting anything.
  • Deleting your account: Settings, Delete account. It is immediate and cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription; cancel that with Apple first.

Premium

  • Premium is bought through the App Store. To cancel or change it, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • If you bought Premium and it is not showing, open Premium in the app and tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

  • Every permission you gave is in Settings, Privacy choices. Turning one off deletes what it produced.
  • Settings, Support and privacy explains what we read from each linked account and what other people can see.
  • Our privacy policy has the whole of it.

Grievance Officer

For complaints about content, conduct, your account or your data, write to our Grievance Officer, Pritika, at pritika@theonebytwo.com. theonebytwo is run by Buddytech India Private Limited.
