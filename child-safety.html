Child safety standards

theonebytwo is a matrimonial app for adults, run by Buddytech India Private Limited. We do not tolerate child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) or child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form. This page sets out how we prevent it and what we do when it is reported.

Only adults

  • theonebytwo is only for people aged 18 or over. Everyone gives their date of birth when they sign up, and the app does not let anyone younger in.
  • On Google Play, the app is listed for adults only, and Google blocks users it has determined to be under 18.
  • An account found to belong to someone under 18 is removed, and its data deleted.

What is not allowed

Any content or behaviour that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child is banned, including sharing, requesting or linking to CSAM, grooming, and sexual messages or images involving anyone under 18. Accounts that do any of this are removed at once and permanently.

How we prevent it

  • Every photo and video sent in a chat is checked for sexual content before it is delivered.
  • Chat messages are checked for abusive words.
  • People can only write to each other once a request is accepted, and anyone can block another person at any time.

How to report it

  • In the app, tap ··· on a profile or in a chat and choose Report. Reporting also blocks the person.
  • Or write to pritika@theonebytwo.com.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, call 112. In India you can also report online child sexual abuse material at cybercrime.gov.in.

What we do with a report

  • Our moderators review reports within 24 hours, and CSAE reports first.
  • We remove the content and the account, and keep what the law requires as evidence.
  • We report child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities, including India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal and the police, as the law requires, and cooperate with their investigations.

Contact

Our designated contact for child safety is Pritika Agarwal, at pritika@theonebytwo.com. She can speak to our CSAE prevention practices and our compliance with child safety laws.
