Privacy policy

Draft, last updated 19 September 2026. This policy describes how the app is built today. It is being reviewed by counsel and will be replaced before public launch.

theonebytwo is a matrimonial app. This policy explains what we collect, what we keep, what we never do, and how you control it. Questions go to __CONTACT_EMAIL__.

1. What you give us directly

  • Your phone number, to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Profile details: first name, date of birth, gender and city. We do not ask for your surname.
  • Photos you upload. We re-create every photo from its pixels before storing it, so location and camera details inside the original file are not kept.
  • Answers you write to profile questions, and who you want to be shown (your age range and which genders).
  • Chat messages, photos and videos you send to people you have matched with.
  • Reports you file about other people.

2. Sources you choose to connect

Every source is optional and asks for its own permission. We read each source once, while your profile is being made. For Google sources we receive a short-lived access key, use it for that one read, then revoke it with Google straight away. We never store it, and we never receive a key that would let us come back later.

YouTube (through Google)

  • We read: the channels you subscribe to, and the videos you have liked, with their channel, topic and category.
  • We do not read: your watch history, your playlists, your comments, or anything you have uploaded.

Gmail (through Google)

  • We read only delivery receipts from Zomato and Swiggy, and delivery emails from Myntra. We find them by searching for those senders; we do not open any other email. We only accept a receipt that Google confirms really came from that company.
  • We keep, per order: the platform, the restaurant or brand, the items and quantities, the date and time, and for food orders the amount paid.
  • We never keep: the email itself, your name, your address, the restaurant's address, your payment method, or any attachment. The email is read in memory and discarded.
  • For Myntra we do not keep sizes or prices, and we leave out whole orders containing items that could reveal something sensitive (for example children's items, maternity, innerwear, religious items, medical or sexual wellness products).

Netflix (a file you upload)

  • You download your own viewing history from one Netflix profile and upload it. We keep the title and date of each view. We refuse Netflix's full account download, because it holds the viewing of everyone on the account.

Spotify (a file you upload)

  • You download your own listening history from Spotify and upload it. We keep the artist, the track, when it played and for how long.
  • We drop, before storing anything, the IP address, country, device and username that Spotify includes with each play. Podcasts, audiobooks and private-session listening are left out.

3. How we use it

  • Your profile. We count things in your data (for example your most-played artist, or how many different shows you have watched) and show them to you as insights. You choose which ones appear on your profile.
  • Matching. We suggest people to you, and you to them, using your preferences and patterns in your data. Insights you hide from your profile can still inform matching, and we say so on that screen. For food, Netflix, Spotify and shopping, matching uses only behaviour (for example how often or how late), never which dishes, shows, artists or brands.
  • Safety. Reviewing reports, and keeping scams and abuse off the app.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data to train AI models.

4. AI suggestions (optional, separate consent)

If you agree, we use an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) to suggest which insights might be interesting to count. We send it names with counts only: for example channel names, restaurant and dish names, show names, artist and track names, and brand names. We never send it your name, contact details, email content, amounts paid or order numbers.

The AI never writes a number or fact about you. It only suggests what to count; our own code does the counting. You can withdraw this consent at any time and the AI-suggested insights are removed.

5. What we will not infer

Food, music and viewing can hint at religion, caste, sexual orientation, health or income. We build to avoid this: data that points at those topics is left out of insights and matching entirely, and we never compute or show such conclusions about you.

6. Who else handles your data

  • Our cloud hosting provider, which stores the app's data.
  • An SMS provider, which sends your sign-in code.
  • Google Firebase, which delivers notifications. A notification carries the other person's first name and fixed text such as "Sent you a message", never what was written.
  • Anthropic, only if you agree to AI suggestions (section 4).

We share data with authorities only when Indian law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Disconnecting a source deletes what we kept from it, and the insights and matching signals made from it.
  • Unmatching closes the chat for both people. It is deleted 14 days later, unless someone has reported the conversation, in which case it is kept until the report is resolved.
  • Deleting your account is immediate and permanent. If a report against you is still open, your account is closed straight away but kept until the report is resolved, then deleted.
  • After deletion we keep a record that consent was given and withdrawn (which notice, and when), with nothing that identifies you, as proof we asked. We also keep a security log of actions taken, which holds no names, phone numbers or content.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act you can ask what we hold about you, correct it, have it erased, withdraw any consent, and complain. You can withdraw consent or delete your account in the app, or write to our Grievance Officer at __CONTACT_EMAIL__. We will reply within the time the law sets.

9. Age

theonebytwo is only for people aged 18 or over.

10. Google user data

theonebytwo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to build the profile features described above, which you can see in the app. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not let people read it except with your permission, for security, or where the law requires, and we do not use it to train AI models.

11. Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will tell you in the app and ask again where the law requires it.
