theonebytwo

Meet someone who gets your taste.

theonebytwo is a matrimonial app for people who would rather be known by what they listen to, watch and order at 1am than by a form. Connect the apps you already use, see what they say about you, and choose what goes on your profile.

Coming soon, starting in Delhi NCR.

How it works

  • You choose which sources to connect: YouTube, Spotify, Netflix, and food and shopping receipts from Gmail. Each one is optional and asks for its own permission.
  • We read each source once, while your profile is being made. We never keep access to your accounts.
  • You pick which insights appear on your profile. You can disconnect a source, or delete your account, at any time.

Read our privacy policy for exactly what we read, what we keep, and what we never do.
