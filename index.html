Why theonebytwo

A biodata tells you their height. Not whether you’ll get along.

Matrimonial profiles today are forms: caste, salary, height, a stiff photo. People fill them in the same way, and they all start to look alike. What makes two people click lives somewhere else: in the songs on repeat, the shows they finish, the food they order late at night.

A typical matrimonial profile

  • A form you fill in once and forget
  • Everyone describes themselves the same way
  • Matches on salary, height and surname
  • Little proof of who is real

theonebytwo

  • A profile built from what you actually love
  • Insights that are true because they come from your own apps
  • Matches on taste, habits and what you are looking for
  • LinkedIn on every profile, with optional DigiLocker verification

How it works

Your profile, in three steps.

No long questionnaire. The apps you already use know you better than any form.

1

Connect what you like

Choose which sources to connect: YouTube, Spotify, Netflix, and food, shopping and travel receipts from Gmail. Each one is optional and asks for its own permission.

2

See what they say about you

We read each source once and turn it into insights: your most-played artist, the videos you love, how late you order dinner. You pick which ones go on your profile.

3

Meet people who get it

Get matched on taste and on what you are looking for. Send a request with a line of your own, chat once you match, and share a profile with family when you are ready.

Privacy and safety

Built to be trusted with something personal.

We ask for a lot when we ask you to connect your apps, so privacy is part of the product, not a page at the end. Our privacy policy says exactly what we read, what we keep and what we never do.

✓

Read once, never kept

Each source is read once while your profile is made. We never keep access to your accounts, and never store your emails.

✓

You choose what shows

Every insight is yours to show or hide, and every connection can be taken back in Settings, deleting what it made.

✓

No ads, no selling

We never sell your data and never show ads. The business is Premium, paid by the people who use it.

✓

Nothing sensitive inferred

Data that could point to religion, caste, health, sexuality or politics is left out of insights and matching entirely.

✓

Real people only

LinkedIn on every profile and optional verification through DigiLocker. Reports are reviewed by moderators within 24 hours.

✓

Leave whenever you want

Take a break, or delete your account in the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

AI, used carefully

Our code counts. Claude writes the words.

Raw data is not a profile. theonebytwo uses Claude, by Anthropic, to turn the patterns in your connected apps into short, human captions, and to suggest which insights say the most about you.

  • Optional, with its own permission that you can turn off at any time.
  • Claude sees summaries only: never your name, number, photos, messages or emails.
  • Every number is computed by our own code. The AI never makes up a fact about you.
  • Your data is never used to train AI models.
