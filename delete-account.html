Delete your account

theonebytwo is run by Buddytech India Private Limited. You can delete your theonebytwo account, and the data that goes with it, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open theonebytwo and go to You, Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account, then Delete everything, and confirm.

It is immediate and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Write to pritika@theonebytwo.com from any address, with the subject "Delete my account" and the phone number you signed up with. We will text a code to that number to make sure the request is yours, then delete the account and tell you when it is done, within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, date of birth, gender, city and everything else on it, your photos and videos, and your answers.
  • Your chats, requests, matches, saved profiles and who you viewed.
  • Everything read from accounts you connected (YouTube, Gmail, Netflix, Spotify, Apple Music), and the insights and matching signals made from it.
  • Your phone number, your devices and your notification token.

What is kept, and for how long

  • If a report against you is open, your account is closed at once but kept until the report is resolved, then deleted.
  • A record that each permission was given and taken back (which text, and when), with nothing that identifies you, as proof we asked.
  • A security log of actions that holds no names, numbers or content.
  • Apple and Google keep their own records of any Premium purchase.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it first in your Apple ID's Subscriptions on iPhone, or in the Play Store under Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions on Android.
